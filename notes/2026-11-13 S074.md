**The brief**: picods102 was moving office → garden (user-powered shutdown
+ restart). Two questions: (1) does "boots on a monitor but not on the

**The diagnosis answer (no — config loss is the LEAST likely cause)**, as a
ladder written into the runbook: (1) DHCP re-addressed the node (102's
lease is NOT reserved — this bit us at S069's rebuild, `.54`→`.57`); (2)
mDNS takes ~20–40 s to register after boot — ping the IP, not the name;
(3) `fsck.repair=yes` repairs a dirty rootfs BEFORE the node comes up — on
a worn card that takes minutes and looks exactly like "booting but
unreachable"; (4) genuinely lost wpa_supplicant/config — rare, last resort.
The same ladder is listed in the runbook as the outage triage order.

**THE CALLHOME FINDING (bigger than the brief)**: the estate ALREADY sends
its health to imagebank — `callhome.sh` POSTs to `POST /node-report` on
starting/stopping/no-go + a ~5-min rate-limited ping (via `run-from-cron.sh`),
and the bank stores `node_state` (one row per node, last word) +
`node_events` (the full trail, 20k+ rows) in **`harvest-v2.db`** (the
`-dev` name is historical; the LIVE database). Verified all three nodes
reporting (~10:46–10:49, `throttled=0x0` everywhere) — my live checks
confirmed it only after a detour (see Observations). For outage triage this
is now THE first surface: a clean reboot = pings then `starting`; a power
cut = a **ping gap with NO stopping event** (the node never said goodbye);
a slow fsck death = a long gap then late `starting`. Facts per ping include
`throttled` (PSU evidence), `boot_id`, and `prev_boot_tail` (the previous
boot's last words — needs the persistent journal; picods_100 posts it,
101/102 sampled without **[unverified]**). Open gap: nothing ALERTS on a
heartbeat gap — candidate bank-side follow-up.
