## S034    2026-10-26 (night)    reception ingest: nodes push to a pool, imagebank drains it

**The user's asks**: frequent small-bite ingestion so new images register in the DB; a reception area so the ingest has a clear pool of work (instead of nodes pushing into final resting places); the ingest owns register + move; is a config page worth it? Decisions: **status + tunables page**, **both nodes' movers switched now**, **5-minute cadence**.

**Design**: nodes push pairs to `/yermo/animals-count/reception/<CAM>(-CLASSIFIED)/<day>/   ` (same sub-path shape as before, so the ingest's move is a straight `os.replace`   " `/yermo` is local on imagebank). `ingest.py` scans the pool (the pool **is** the work list   " "how do we know what's new" answered by construction), skips files younger than the settle window (CIFS copy in progress), registers in `harvest.db` (the same `INSERT..ON CONFLICT` as the historical indexer   " idempotent by natural keys), then moves. **Crash ordering: register+commit before move**   " a mid-bite crash leaves a registered file in reception that the next bite just moves. Assessment-graph XMLs skipped (`model_versions`, harvest.py's rule). Tunables + counters in `scan_state` (hot). `ingest-from-cron.sh` wraps a bite + `workflow_state.py` refresh under flocks (the assess idiom), cron `*/5`.
